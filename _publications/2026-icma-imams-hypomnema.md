Proposes a critical reconstruction of sīrah and tafsīr narratives from al-Bāqir and al-Ṣādiq using Isnād-cum-Matn Analysis, and introduces the Imām's hypomnēma as a mnemonic-textual interface between oral instruction and written transmission.
